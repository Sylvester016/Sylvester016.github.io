Impact & Outcomes

Building Confidence, Access, and Employability

Our work is grounded in measurable outcomes: better academic performance, higher rates of bursary applications, and stronger career readiness among UL students in SMCS and across FSA.

5+ high‑risk modules supported

From foundational mathematics to advanced computing, our extra classes and cross-night sessions target modules with historically high failure rates.

5 weeks continuous bursary support

Faculty-wide bursary drives help students navigate complex funding processes and meet strict deadlines with confidence.

100+ students engaged annually

Our programmes attract students from diverse SMCS disciplines, creating a collaborative learning culture across the faculty.

Certificates earned through FNB Academy

Participation in external academies and seminars ensures our members graduate with both academic depth and industry-relevant skills.
