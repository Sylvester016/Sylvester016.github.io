Our Activities

High-Impact Academic & Career Initiatives

We design and deliver structured programmes that respond directly to the academic and financial realities faced by UL students, especially in high-risk mathematical and computing modules.

Joint Opening Function

Collaborative welcome for SMCS & FSA students

In partnership with PMSSS and MLSSA, UL ASCOMS co-hosted a joint opening function, introducing societies, academic support structures, and expectations to first-year and returning students, with dedicated budgets for student-focused catering.

  • Early exposure to academic support and mentoring

Faculty Bursary Drive

5-week support for funding applications

UL ASCOMS led a faculty-wide bursary drive to assist students with NSFAS and external bursary applications, focusing on accurate documentation, motivation letters, and deadline management.

  • 5 weeks of structured support across FSA
  • Improved access to financial aid opportunities

Extra Classes & Tutorials

Free academic support for high‑risk modules

We coordinate free extra classes, revision sessions, and tutorial support for modules such as SMTH000/011/012, SSTA021/022, SCOA021/022/031/032, and SMTA021/022/031, open to all qualifying students.

  • Student-friendly explanations and problem walkthroughs
  • Peer-led and lecturer-supported sessions

Peer Learning & Cross-Nights

Safe, focused spaces for deep study

We promote structured peer-led learning and host intensive cross-night study sessions with refreshments funded by the executive, enabling students to revise together in a supportive environment.

  • Executive-funded coffee and snacks
  • Guided revision with senior students

FNB Academy Participation

Professional certificates for employability

UL ASCOMS members participated in the FNB Academy, gaining short professional certifications that demonstrate workplace skills and enhance graduate employability in the financial and technology sectors.

  • Practical exposure to industry expectations
  • CV-strengthening certificates earned by students

AI & 4IR Seminar

Future-focused perspectives on AI in South Africa

In collaboration with PAISA Technology, we hosted an AI seminar exploring artificial intelligence, data, and Fourth Industrial Revolution careers, tailored for UL students in mathematics and computing.

  • Insights into AI, data, and tech roles
  • Discussion of ethical and African-context issues
