About UL ASCOMS

Academic Representation for Mathematical & Computer Sciences

UL ASCOMS is the official student society representing students in Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Sciences, Operational Research, and Mathematics at the University of Limpopo. We exist to enhance student life, academic outcomes, welfare, and career readiness.

Our Mission

To serve as the primary academic representative body for students in the School of Mathematical and Computer Sciences, advocating for quality teaching, inclusive support, and equitable access to opportunities across the Faculty of Science and Agriculture.

Our Vision

A community of UL students who are confident, industry-ready problem solvers, using mathematics and computing to build sustainable solutions for Africa and beyond.

Our Role

We bridge students, faculty, and institutional structures such as the SRC to promote academic excellence, transparent governance, and a culture of peer-led learning within SMCS.
