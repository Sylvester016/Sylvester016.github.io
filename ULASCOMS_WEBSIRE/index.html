About UL ASCOMS

Academic Representation for Mathematical & Computer Sciences

UL ASCOMS is the official student society representing students in Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Sciences, Operational Research, and Mathematics at the University of Limpopo. We exist to enhance student life, academic outcomes, welfare, and career readiness.

Our Mission

To serve as the primary academic representative body for students in the School of Mathematical and Computer Sciences, advocating for quality teaching, inclusive support, and equitable access to opportunities across the Faculty of Science and Agriculture.

Our Vision

A community of UL students who are confident, industry-ready problem solvers, using mathematics and computing to build sustainable solutions for Africa and beyond.

Our Role

We bridge students, faculty, and institutional structures such as the SRC to promote academic excellence, transparent governance, and a culture of peer-led learning within SMCS.

Our Activities

High-Impact Academic & Career Initiatives

We design and deliver structured programmes that respond directly to the academic and financial realities faced by UL students, especially in high-risk mathematical and computing modules.

Joint Opening Function

Collaborative welcome for SMCS & FSA students

In partnership with PMSSS and MLSSA, UL ASCOMS co-hosted a joint opening function, introducing societies, academic support structures, and expectations to first-year and returning students, with dedicated budgets for student-focused catering.

  • Early exposure to academic support and mentoring

Faculty Bursary Drive

5-week support for funding applications

UL ASCOMS led a faculty-wide bursary drive to assist students with NSFAS and external bursary applications, focusing on accurate documentation, motivation letters, and deadline management.

  • 5 weeks of structured support across FSA
  • Improved access to financial aid opportunities

Extra Classes & Tutorials

Free academic support for high‑risk modules

We coordinate free extra classes, revision sessions, and tutorial support for modules such as SMTH000/011/012, SSTA021/022, SCOA021/022/031/032, and SMTA021/022/031, open to all qualifying students.

  • Student-friendly explanations and problem walkthroughs
  • Peer-led and lecturer-supported sessions

Peer Learning & Cross-Nights

Safe, focused spaces for deep study

We promote structured peer-led learning and host intensive “cross-night” study sessions with refreshments funded by the executive, enabling students to revise together in a supportive environment.

  • Executive-funded coffee and snacks
  • Guided revision with senior students

FNB Academy Participation

Professional certificates for employability

UL ASCOMS members participated in the FNB Academy, gaining short professional certifications that demonstrate workplace skills and enhance graduate employability in the financial and technology sectors.

  • Practical exposure to industry expectations
  • CV-strengthening certificates earned by students

AI & 4IR Seminar

Future-focused perspectives on AI in South Africa

In collaboration with PAISA Technology, we hosted an AI seminar exploring artificial intelligence, data, and Fourth Industrial Revolution careers, tailored for UL students in mathematics and computing.

  • Insights into AI, data, and tech roles
  • Discussion of ethical and African-context issues

Impact & Outcomes

Building Confidence, Access, and Employability

Our work is grounded in measurable outcomes: better academic performance, higher rates of bursary applications, and stronger career readiness among UL students in SMCS and across FSA.

5+ high‑risk modules supported

From foundational mathematics to advanced computing, our extra classes and cross-night sessions target modules with historically high failure rates.

5 weeks continuous bursary support

Faculty-wide bursary drives help students navigate complex funding processes and meet strict deadlines with confidence.

100+ students engaged annually

Our programmes attract students from diverse SMCS disciplines, creating a collaborative learning culture across the faculty.

Certificates earned through FNB Academy

Participation in external academies and seminars ensures our members graduate with both academic depth and industry-relevant skills.

Get Involved

Become a Member of UL ASCOMS

Whether you are an undergraduate or postgraduate student in SMCS or a related field within the Faculty of Science and Agriculture, there is a place for you in UL ASCOMS.

Who Can Join?

Membership is open to students registered in Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Sciences, Operational Research, Mathematics, and related programmes within SMCS and FSA.

Why Join?

  • Access to extra classes, cross-night sessions, and study groups
  • Information on bursaries, internships, and graduate programmes
  • Opportunities to lead projects and represent your peers

Membership & SRC Registration

To maintain SRC-recognised status, a minimum of 100 registered affiliates is required. By joining UL ASCOMS, you help secure a strong, accountable student voice for SMCS at UL.
